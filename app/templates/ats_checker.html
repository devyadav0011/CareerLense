What Applicant Tracking Systems Look For

Over 75% of resumes are screened by automated ATS software before a human recruiter reviews them.

Standard Section Headings

ATS parsers search for standard terminology like "Work Experience", "Education", and "Skills". Creative headers like "My Journey" frequently confuse automated screeners.

Clean Text Hierarchy

Tables, complex multi-column layouts, and images can break text extraction. Single-column or clean two-column structures parse reliably.

Keyword Alignment

ATS scanners index technical skills, certifications, and industry tools. CareerLense verifies that your hard skills are formatted in recognized taxonomies.

ATS Best Practices Checklist

How to Ensure 90%+ ATS Pass Rate

Follow these guidelines when building your resume in CareerLense to maximize ATS readability.

Use standard fonts such as Inter, Calibri, Helvetica, or Arial.
Never embed essential text inside images, charts, or SVG elements.
Include clear date ranges (Month Year – Present) for each position.
Provide complete contact details: email, phone, location, and LinkedIn URL.
Quantify bullet points with numbers, percentages, and metrics.
